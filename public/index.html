Biometric Devices

Connect eSSL / ZKTeco devices so attendance flows into the portal automatically.
ADMS push (recommended): the device sends attendance to this portal over the internet by itself. It works from any city and needs no computer at the device's office. LAN pull: for devices without ADMS; a small sync agent on a PC at the device's office uploads the data. In both cases each employee's Biometric ID must match the ID enrolled on the device.

ADMS setup on the device

Policy Agreement

This text is used whenever you generate a new onboarding agreement for an employee (Agreements page). You can use placeholders such as {{employee_name}}, {{employee_code}}, {{designation}}, {{department}}, {{branch}}, {{reporting_manager}}, {{joining_date}}, {{company_name}}, {{company_address}}, {{issue_date}}, {{basic_monthly}}, {{hra_monthly}}, {{other_allowances_monthly}}, {{monthly_ctc}} and {{annual_ctc}}; they are filled in for each employee. Editing it only affects future agreements, not ones already in progress.
